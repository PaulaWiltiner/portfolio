Profile

An engineer who turns problems into optimized, low-cost solutions.

// Hi — I'm Paula.

I'm an AI & software engineer who works end to end — from architecture and cloud back-end to agents, automation and the observability that keeps them running. I build products that combine LLM agents (Claude), AWS data pipelines and operational automation.

What drives me isn't just finding the root cause — it's solving it well. I look for the solution that's optimized and low-cost, not the quickest patch: one normalization layer over N point-fixes, redundant telemetry cut instead of tolerated (FinOps), an event-driven design that scales. Over three years I grew into the technical reference for the AI layer of SEK's internal products.

I move naturally between architecture, back-end integrations, prompt engineering, BI and UI prototyping — translating business needs into software, mapping flows and owning documentation. I work across teams in Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Peru and Argentina, in three languages.
